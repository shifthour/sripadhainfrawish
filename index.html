01 About Us

The city's new chapter is unfolding,
let's shape it together.

The right Land. Location. People.

Sripadha Infrawish brings together like-minded investors to identify, evaluate and participate in strategically located land and real-estate opportunities in and around Bengaluru.

Strategic land parcels are often out of reach for a single buyer, and hard to evaluate without local knowledge. We solve both — combining on-ground location intelligence with a disciplined, documentation-first process, and the collective strength of an investor community that shares a long-term vision.

09 FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

We identify, evaluate and structure strategically located land and real-estate opportunities in and around Bengaluru, and bring together like-minded investors to participate in them collectively.

Individually, a strategic land parcel may be out of reach for a single investor. Through collective participation, a group of investors comes together for a specific opportunity. Each opportunity has its own structure, documentation, terms and applicable legal requirements, which are shared transparently before participation.

Opportunities span land parcels, apartments and villas — primarily across East and South-East Bengaluru growth corridors such as Whitefield, Gunjur, Sarjapur and surrounding areas.

We evaluate connectivity, existing and proposed infrastructure, employment catchments, development momentum and long-term growth indicators before shortlisting any location.

Due diligence covers ownership history, title documentation, encumbrances, land classification, approvals and applicable legal requirements — coordinated with legal professionals before any opportunity moves forward.

Registered investors receive the opportunity's details, structure, documentation and terms before any decision — and are encouraged to review everything and seek independent advice.

Professionals, business owners and individuals interested in land, apartment and villa opportunities can register. Registration simply expresses interest — there is no obligation to participate in any opportunity.

Yes. NRIs form an important part of our investor community. Participation for NRIs is subject to applicable regulations, and our team guides you through the specifics for each opportunity.

We welcome conversations with landowners and developers for outright acquisition, joint development, land aggregation and development partnerships. Use the "Talk to Our Land Team" form to start a discussion.

Participation levels vary by opportunity, based on its size and structure. Register your interest and preferred range, and our team will share opportunities that fit.
